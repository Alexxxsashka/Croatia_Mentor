# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack
Next.js 16 (App Router), React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS v4, Prisma ORM (PostgreSQL), NextAuth v5, next-intl (i18n), Google GenAI (@google/genai), edge-tts-universal.

## Users
Ukrainian relocators seeking temporary protection and integration in Croatia, alongside expats, digital nomads, and general language enthusiasts across English, Russian, and Ukrainian speaking backgrounds who need practical Croatian for daily life, residency, and communication.

## Product Purpose
Empower learners to achieve functional and fluent Croatian proficiency through structured CEFR-aligned lessons, spaced-repetition vocabulary training, interactive AI conversation, and audio dictation.

## Positioning
An immersive, AI-powered Croatian language mentor combining structured CEFR curriculum (A1-C2), SM-2 spaced-repetition flashcards, intelligent audio/chat interaction, and a distinctive dark editorial & adventure brand identity.

## Operating Context
Daily learning micro-sessions (5-15 mins) and deep-dive modules across desktop and mobile web browsers, supporting real-time language switching (EN, RU, UA), progress tracking via XP, streaks, badges, and review queues.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- CEFR level progression tracking (A1 through C2) with initial Placement Test.
- Spaced Repetition System (SRS) using SM-2 algorithm (`easeFactor`, review intervals, word status).
- Interactive AI Tutor & Dictation powered by Google GenAI and Edge TTS.
- Multi-lingual localization (`next-intl`) supporting English, Russian, and Ukrainian interfaces.
- Core learning surfaces: Placement Test, Grammar/Reading/Dictation lessons, Flashcards, Games, AI Chat, User Dashboard, Profile, Glossary, Admin.
- Gamification elements: XP, daily streaks, unlocked badges, custom goal targets.
- Dark Editorial & Adventure design system specified in `DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: Croatia Mentor
- Visual Identity: Dark Editorial & Adventure theme defined in `DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` featuring deep graphite surfaces (`#0B0D10`), warm bronze accents (`#C88A4B`), high-energy red markers (`#FF3B30`), high tracking headers, thin 1px linear grid lines, and Playfair Display typography.
- Tone of Voice: Sophisticated, empowering, structured, and adventurous.

## Product Principles
1. **Practical Fluency First**: Focus on high-frequency vocabulary, real-life situational dialogues, and audio comprehension for rapid integration.
2. **Scientific Spaced Retention**: Utilize SRS (SM-2) active recall algorithms to turn short-term practice into permanent vocabulary.
3. **Editorial Adventure Atmosphere**: Elevate language study into an inspiring journey using dramatic typography, dark surfaces, and refined visual craft.
4. **Multi-lingual Inclusivity**: Provide seamless language switching across EN, RU, and UA to support diverse relocator demographics.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Multi-lingual interface options (EN, RU, UA), high-contrast readability on dark surfaces (WCAG AA), responsive layout adaptation across mobile and desktop devices, and semantic HTML structure.
